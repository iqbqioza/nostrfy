How to Open a Door

A short guide for anyone standing in front of one.

  1. Open the "Door opener's toolkit" below.
  2. Press the Copy button.

Door opener's toolkit

About this guide

Door opening is a timeless skill. People have opened doors for thousands of years, and this guide collects the essential technique in one place so that no one is ever stuck in front of one again.

Frequently asked questions

Push or pull?

Yes.

What if the door has no handle?

Congratulations, you have found a wall. There is no step for that.

What about sliding doors?

Slide it. If it does not slide, see the wall answer above.

Do I really have to close it?

Only if you enjoy heating the outdoors.
